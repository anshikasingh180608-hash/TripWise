# TripWise: Project Statement

## Problem Statement
When planning a journey, travelers frequently struggle to keep their information organized[cite: 1]. Important details such as destination research, daily itineraries, and financial tracking are often scattered across multiple platforms—such as digital notes, calendars, and spreadsheets. This fragmented approach makes it difficult to maintain a clear overview of the trip's schedule and frequently results in overspending due to a lack of real-time budget monitoring.

## Scope of the Project
The scope of this project encompasses building a unified, console-based Java application that consolidates the core elements of travel planning[cite: 1]. The system manages the full lifecycle of trip preparation: logging desired destinations, scheduling travel dates, building day-by-day itineraries, and tracking expenses against a defined budget cap. The application operates entirely offline, utilizing local CSV file handling to ensure data persistence between user sessions.

## Target Users
* **Independent Travelers & Backpackers:** Individuals who need a simple, offline tool to track their daily spending and schedule[cite: 1].
* **Student Groups:** College students managing restricted travel budgets for weekend getaways who need a strict system to prevent overspending.
* **Trip Organizers:** Planners looking to maintain a clear, unified itinerary and expense log without relying on complex spreadsheet formulas[cite: 1].

## High-Level Features
* **Destination Management:** A centralized repository to add, search, view, and remove travel locations[cite: 1].
* **Trip Scheduling & Budgeting:** Tools to create overarching trips with defined start/end dates and strict financial limits[cite: 1].
* **Itinerary Building:** The ability to link specific daily activities and notes to a designated trip[cite: 1].
* **Automated Expense Tracking:** A financial module that logs individual transactions and dynamically calculates the remaining budget to provide real-time status warnings (e.g., "Within Budget" or "Budget Exceeded")[cite: 1].
